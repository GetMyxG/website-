How it works

  1. Upload your match

    Filmed on a phone, a GoPro or a club camera like Veo. If you can see the pitch, it works.

  2. Tap your player

    Show us who you are and confirm your goals and assists, so the report is about you and gets the big moments right.

  3. Get your report and card

    xG, shots, chances created, dribbles, tackles and a match rating, plus a share card for your socials.
